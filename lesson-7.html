Has Got or Have Got?

I / you / we / they— have got· haven't got
he / she / it— has got· hasn't got
Simba has got a blue collar. Tonya and Simba have got a picture book. The mice have got small feet.
Помни: children, men, women, mice, teeth, feet, people, geese — это «много», значит have got!

Выбери правильную форму.
